About the packaging mockup & dieline tool

Set your box style and dimensions and the tool draws a print-ready dieline to exact millimetres — cut lines, fold creases and a 3 mm bleed — right in your browser, so it’s instant and free. Then generate a photorealistic 3D mockup of the finished box in your brand colours to show clients or use in listings.

The dieline is a starting template; always confirm final cutter specs with your printer before production.
