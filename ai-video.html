About the video voiceover tool

Upload a short video, pick a character voice, and either type what the character should say or convert your own recorded voice into the character's voice (speech-to-speech). The tool builds the voiceover and lets you preview it over your video and download a finished file — all in your browser. You can also generate standalone character clips (talking, cooking, walking) to cut into your video. Voice features use ElevenLabs and need a paid plan for cloning and speech-to-speech.
